Covered
Covered Call Automation Platform

Covered is our flagship web application that brings broker-connected covered call strategy execution to your fingertips. Connect your trading account, set your parameters, and let the platform handle the options overlay — systematically and seamlessly.

  • Direct broker account integration via secure API
  • Automated covered call writing on your equity holdings
  • Real-time position monitoring & P&L dashboard
  • Configurable strike selection and expiry preferences
  • Roll-forward and exit management with one click
  • Multi-segment support: NSE equity & F&O

DataEdge

About Us

Who We Are

A fintech firm born from the conviction that systematic options strategies should be accessible to every serious investor.

Zerodelta Algrow LLP was founded on a simple but powerful idea: the covered call strategy — one of the most reliably income-generating options techniques used by institutions — was needlessly complex and inaccessible for individual investors and portfolio managers in India.

We set out to change that. By combining deep market expertise with modern software engineering, we built platforms that automate the grunt work, surface the right data, and let our clients focus on what matters most — making better decisions.

Today, Zerodelta Algrow serves clients ranging from high-net-worth individuals to boutique family offices, providing them with tools that were previously the exclusive domain of proprietary trading desks.

"Our philosophy is simple: zero in on the delta between where retail investors are and where they could be — and close that gap with smarter algorithms and sustainable growth."
